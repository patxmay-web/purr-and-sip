The Story Behind Purr & Sip

Hi! I'm Patricia May, a college student who loves cats, cozy cafés, and discovering relaxing places to study and unwind. I created Purr & Sip to build a curated space for fellow animal lovers who are searching for peaceful spots in the city where good coffee meets friendly feline companionship.

Why trust my recommendations? Every café featured in this guide is handpicked and evaluated based on cat welfare, store cleanliness, peaceful vibe, and high-quality drinks. I make sure that every place I recommend promotes ethical animal treatment while offering a relaxing environment for guests!
