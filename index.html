Welcome to Purr & Sip!

About Me

Hi! I'm Patricia May, a college student who loves cats, cozy cafés, and discovering relaxing places. I created Purr & Sip to share places where people can enjoy a cup of coffee while spending time with adorable and friendly cats.

My Top Picks

Catopia Café

Location: Quezon City

I recommend this place because of its calm atmosphere, cozy seating, and friendly rescue cats that love cuddling while you enjoy your coffee.

The Pawsome Lounge

Location: Makati City

A great spot for studying or relaxing. They have delicious pastries, great iced lattes, and very energetic kitties that bring a lot of joy.

Whiskers & Brews

Location: Pasig City

Perfect for coffee lovers and cat lovers alike. The staff is welcoming, the coffee is top quality, and the cats are extremely well cared for.
